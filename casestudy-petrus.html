Petrus

Advanced IoT Energy Analytics & High-Density Dashboards

IoT Analytics Information Architecture Dark Mode UI
Tools Used Figma | D3.js | Slack
Timeline 12 Weeks

Petrus tracks real-time high-density energy logs for heavy manufacturing industries. By designing a high-contrast dark theme dashboard, we transformed confusing rows of live telemetry data into actionable, visual intelligence that saved operators 400+ hours.

The Challenge

Factory technicians and operators deal with dozens of live gauges, thermal sensors, and high-voltage grid readings simultaneously. The original Petrus interface looked like a legacy Excel spreadsheet—bright white, visually exhausting, and prone to alert fatigue. When anomalies occurred, engineers struggled to pinpoint the failure point in real-time, resulting in expensive energy waste.

The Solution

Glare-Reduction Dark UI:

We developed an dark-themed interface specifically optimized for high-glare control rooms, preventing eye fatigue during long 12-hour shifts.

Proactive Alert Triaging:

We designed a color-coded warning hierarchy based on urgency. Low-priority noise is suppressed, and critical grid failures are highlighted instantly to prompt fast actions.

Anomalies Identification:

We integrated dynamic zoomable brush-selection timelines that let operators isolate energy surges and track root-cause devices in under 10 seconds.

Key Features

Petrus transforms dense industrial telemetry into visual intelligence. The interface was built for operators who can't afford to misread data — every pixel serves a purpose, from micro-indicators to full-screen anomaly overlays.

Dark Theme UI

High-contrast dark mode calibrated for control rooms and extended 12-hour shifts.

Zoomable Timelines

Brush-selection time controls let operators isolate energy spikes in under 10 seconds.

Alert Triage System

Severity-layered alerts surface only critical failures, eliminating alert fatigue entirely.

Live Sensor Grid

Real-time telemetry from 100+ sensors visualized as glanceable heat maps and gauges.
