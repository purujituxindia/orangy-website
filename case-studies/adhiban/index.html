Adhiban

Empowering Financial Growth with Intelligent Solutions

UX Research Product Design Design System
Tools Used Figma | Miro | Story book | Jira
Time Line 10 Weeks

Adhiban serves a diverse spectrum of clients, each with unique financial behaviours, goals, and challenges. Our role was to translate these complex, highly-specific financial needs into intuitive digital workflows that simplify decision-making and build trust.

The Challenge

Athiban approached us with a robust suite of financial products but faced a disconnect in user engagement. Their existing ecosystem was fragmented, making it difficult for users to navigate between wealth management, banking, and insurance. The goal was to transform a complex financial institution into an intuitive, accessible digital partner.

The Solution

Empathy-Driven Research:

We moved beyond surface-level demographics to understand the emotional weight of financial management. Through user interviews and persona mapping, we identified the primary friction points: jargon overload, confusing navigation, and a lack of transparency.

Architecture & Strategy:

To unify Athiban's diverse offerings, we developed a modular information architecture. This allowed us to house Wealth Management, Banking, and Corporate Solutions under one cohesive roof without overwhelming the user.

The Visual Language:

We designed a clean, trust-inspiring interface that leverages whitespace and data visualization. By simplifying complex financial data into digestible "glanceable" metrics, we reduced cognitive load for the user.

Key Features

We focused on a minimalist and clutter-free interface for the retail mobile app. Our approach aimed for simplicity, ensuring a smooth and intuitive user experience. We crafted a logical flow, guiding users effortlessly through the app's navigation. The checkout process was streamlined for flawless usability, maintaining a straightforward design.

Unified Dashboard

A centralized hub providing a 360-degree view of personal wealth and business cash flow.

Smart Onboarding

A friction-free KYC and account setup process designed to increase conversion rates.

Visualized Insights

Replaced static spreadsheets with interactive charts for Investment Advisory and Insurance coverage.

Adaptive Workflows

Dynamic flows that auto-adjust complexity, maximizing efficiency and compliance.

OUTCOMES

Simplified onboarding and intuitive UI led to more users completing sign-up and engaging with the app within the first session.

+40%

Increased Accessibility:

24/7 self-service capabilities reduced customer support dependency.

2x

Enhanced Clarity

Users reported higher confidence in decision-making due to improved data visualization.

30%

Scalable System

A design system that allows Athiban to easily add new products as they grow.

The Process

Final Thoughts

Our design philosophy turned financial complexity into human-centered simplicity. By engineering adaptive workflows and intuitive segmented dashboards, we ensured every user, from the SME founder to the seasoned CEO, experiences Adhiban as their personal, trusted catalyst for growth.
